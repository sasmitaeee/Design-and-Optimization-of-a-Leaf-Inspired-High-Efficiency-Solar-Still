Project Overview
   This project focuses on designing and optimizing a leaf-inspired solar still to improve freshwater production using renewable solar energy.
Objectives
   Compare conventional and leaf-inspired solar still designs.Improve water distribution and heat utilization.Evaluate temperature, thermal efficiency, and freshwater yield.
   Explore a sustainable solution for water desalination.
Working Principle
   Solar energy heats saline or contaminated water, causing evaporation. The water vapor condenses on the glass cover and is collected as freshwater.
Methodology
   A conventional solar still and a proposed leaf-inspired solar still are developed and compared through simulation.
Key Parameters
   Solar Radiation
   Water Temperature
   Absorber Temperature
   Evaporation Rate
   Freshwater Yield
   Thermal Efficiency
Expected Outcome
   The leaf-inspired design aims to improve freshwater production and thermal performance compared with a conventional solar still.
Future Scope
   Prototype fabrication, experimental validation, and performance improvement.
Keywords
   Solar Still, Leaf-Inspired Design, Solar Desalination, Freshwater Production, Renewable Energy.
